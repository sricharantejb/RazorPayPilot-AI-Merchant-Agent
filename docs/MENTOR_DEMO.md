# RazorPayPilot Mentor Demo — 5 Minutes

## 1. Open AI Command Center
Show the live monitor, revenue at risk and Revenue Rescue Mission.

Say: **"Pilot does not wait for the merchant to find the problem. It continuously scans the workspace and turns risk into a bounded mission."**

## 2. Revenue Rescue Mission
Open the mission and show:
- failed-payment recovery
- high-intent cart recovery
- customer win-back
- accessory cross-sell

Run the safe failed-payment step. For actions over the autonomous threshold, show the approval gate instead of bypassing it.

## 3. Ask AI Copilot
Ask:
- Why is revenue at risk?
- Analyze failed payments.
- What should I do today?

The Copilot is a real LLM when a provider key is configured. It is grounded in the included Test Mode merchant context.

## 4. Customer 360
Open Customer 360 and show LTV, RFM, churn risk, purchase probability, personalized recommendations and the customer timeline.

## 5. Revenue Simulator
Change the audience and discount. Run the simulation. Explain that Pilot compares expected recovery with incentive cost before acting.

## 6. Agent Control Room
Show the specialized agents and start a mission. Emphasize that they share context, policy and audit state.

## 7. AI Buyer
Search: **"wireless headphones under ₹3,000"**.
Select a product and use Buy in Test Mode. With Razorpay Test Mode credentials configured, the backend creates a real Test Mode order; otherwise it creates a clearly labeled local Test Mode order.

## 8. Failure Center
Run the failure simulation. Show the safe stop, no-duplicate outcome and audit events.

## 9. Evaluation Lab
Run the fixed benchmark and show zero policy violations, bounded execution and decision latency.

## 10. Finish on Audit Trail
Explain the closed loop:

**Detect → Investigate → Predict → Simulate → Policy → Approval → Act → Verify → Measure → Learn**

Do not describe simulated values as production payments or production recovery.
